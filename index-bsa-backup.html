About

A systems analyst who runs the queries, ships the configs, and aligns the room.

For four years I've sat between business stakeholders and the enterprise systems they depend on — translating pain points into functional and technical requirements, getting hands-on with the platforms to validate before development, and mapping how data actually flows between teams. That's the heart of business systems analysis: making cross-system complexity legible to both sides, then shipping the change.

At Amazon Robotics, I drove an Oracle EBS to SAP S/4HANA transformation across 3 concurrent workstreams — set scope and sequencing across 6 platforms (Coupa, Ariba, BTP, MDG), aligned stakeholders at all levels, and authored the functional spec the SI used as their baseline. I shipped an OAF / APEX supply-demand dashboard that cut PO approval cycle from 15–20 days to 1–2, and built a Python automation that avoided $1.7M in annualized headcount costs. Phil Conlon called my work “freakin awesome” — which I'm choosing to count as a KPI.

Before Amazon, at Wipro, I delivered a full P2P implementation for GUVNL (a state electricity utility) — gathered requirements, designed Oracle AME across 6 approval levels, cut PO cycle from 8 to 3 days, and closed a 2-year-old audit finding on go-live. Authored 40+ functional specifications and built a real-time SQL-driven payment aging dashboard that recovered $0.8M in early-payment discounts. Before that — Business Analyst at HP, where I ran requirements gathering with 10+ cross-functional partners and built Power BI dashboards with custom DAX.

I just graduated from Purdue's Master of Engineering Management program (GPA 3.7, May 2026). PMP-certified, three SAP certifications, two Microsoft Azure (AZ-900, DP-300). Outside work — I deadlift 400+, watch cricket whenever India plays, and have re-read Working Backwards three times now.
